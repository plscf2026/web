# Committee Management Guide
## Police Lines High School & College, Faridpur Reunion 2027

---

## 1. Overview & Organizational Philosophy
The Reunion Management System (RMS) replaces rigid static organizing committee lists with a fully dynamic, database-backed Committee Management Engine. Each of the 12 functional sub-committees operates as an autonomous operational unit with its own budget, roster, task backlog, document folder, and weekly reporting pipeline, all reporting to the Central Steering Committee.

## 3. Standard Operational Workflows

### 3.1 Creating a New Sub-Committee
1. Navigate to **12 Committees Directory** (`RmsCommitteesModule`).
2. Click **Create New Sub-Committee**.
3. Fill in English and Bengali Name, Description, Director Name, Contact details, Meeting Day, and Proposed Budget.
4. Click **Create Sub-Committee**. An audit record is automatically logged.

### 3.2 Adding Members to a Committee
1. Select the committee from the grid and click **View Member Roster**.
2. Click **Add Committee Member**.
3. Provide Member Name, Phone Number, Email, and designated role (`DIRECTOR`, `ASSISTANT_DIRECTOR`, `MEMBER`, or `ADVISER`).
4. The member is added to the committee roster and their contact card is displayed.

### 3.3 Transferring a Member Between Committees
1. In the Member Roster drawer, locate the member and click the **Transfer Member** icon.
2. Select the target committee from the dropdown list.
3. Confirm transfer. The member's task counts and history remain intact while their committee binding shifts.
