# User Manual & Operating Guide
## Police Lines High School & College, Faridpur Reunion 2027

---

## Part A: Public Attendee Guide

### 1. Online Registration & Payment
1. Navigate to the website homepage and click **Register for Reunion**.
2. Select your category:
   - **Current Student**: Enter your verified Institutional Student ID (e.g. `PLSC-2024-0012`), Class, and Section.
   - **Former Student / Alumni**: Enter your Graduation Year (SSC/HSC) and Batch details.
3. Provide your Full Name, 11-digit Bangladeshi Mobile Number, and T-shirt size.
4. **Photo Upload**:
   - Click **Take Photo with Camera** or **Upload Image File**.
   - The system automatically crops and compresses your portrait under 200KB.
5. Click **Proceed to Payment**. You will be securely redirected to the PayStation Bangladesh gateway to pay via bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or Visa/Mastercard.
6. Upon successful payment, your cryptographically signed e-Ticket will be generated with a high-resolution QR pass.

## Part B: Gate & Distribution Staff Guide

### 1. Multi-Station QR Verification Desk
1. Open the website on your tablet, phone, or laptop and click **Scanner Portal** in the top navigation.
2. Select your assigned duty station:
   - **Gate 01 / Gate 02 Entrance Scanner**: Scans attendee QR code. Verifies valid registration, checks entry status, and flags duplicate entrance attempts.
   - **Food Pavilion Scanner**: Scans QR code to verify and disburse meal tokens for lunch banquet. Prevents double collection.
   - **Dress & Kit Bag Counter**: Verifies delegate registration, records T-shirt size issuance, and marks collection.
3. If camera permission is blocked, you can manually type the attendee's Ticket ID (e.g. `PLSCF-2026-F8941`) to verify.

## Part C: Central Leadership Operating Guide
*(Convener Monowar Shikder & Member Secretary Siddiqur Rahman)*

### 1. Navigating to the ERP Portal
- Click **RMS Portal** in the website header or footer.
- Log in using your email and the universal administrative PIN `admin@123`.

### 2. Approving Committee Budgets
1. Click **Finance & Accounts** from the left navigation.
2. Under **Budget Requisitions Pipeline**, review requests marked `RECOMMENDED` by the Finance Desk Officer.
3. Click **Review Requisition**. Confirm or adjust the allocated figure and click **Approve Budget**.
4. The approved funds are immediately reflected in the committee's allocated balance.

### 3. Evaluating Weekly Committee Reports
1. Click **Weekly Progress Reports**.
2. Inspect the latest week's submission from each of the 12 committee directors.
3. Review work completed, pending bottlenecks, and central support requests.
4. Click **Conduct Central Review** and either **Approve Report** or **Request Revision** with written directives.

## Part D: Committee Director Operating Guide
*(Director Sagar - Registration & Database, and all 11 Sub-Committee Directors)*

### 1. Managing Tasks & Assigning Responsibilities
1. Click **Task Management**.
2. Toggle between **Kanban Board**, **Table**, or **Calendar** views.
3. Click **Create New Task** to assign action items to committee members.
4. When a member requests review, inspect their submitted evidence and click **Approve & Mark Completed**.

### 2. Requesting Operational Budget
1. Click **Finance & Accounts** -> **Submit Budget Requisition**.
2. Enter the title, expense category, requested Taka amount, and vendor quotation details.
3. Submit for Finance Desk vetting.

### 3. Submitting Weekly Progress Reports
1. At the end of each operational week, navigate to **Weekly Progress Reports**.
2. Click **Submit Weekly Report**.
3. Enumerate completed tasks, ongoing items, expenditures, and central assistance required.

## Part E: Event Day Field Operations
*(January 28-29, 2027)*

### 1. Volunteer Shift Check-In / Check-Out
1. Duty supervisors access **Volunteer Corps**.
2. When a volunteer arrives at Gate 01, Food Pavilion, or Stage, click **Check-In Shift**.
3. When the shift concludes, click **Check-Out Shift**.

### 2. Emergency Incident Reporting & Dispatch
1. If an issue occurs (Security, Medical, Logistics, Lost & Found), click **Event Day Control** -> **Report Incident**.
2. Select Severity (`LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`), enter exact location and description.
3. The assigned quick-response team is dispatched and resolution notes are logged once handled.
