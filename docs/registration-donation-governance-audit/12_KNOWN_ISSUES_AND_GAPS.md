## 1. Critical Architectural & Governance Bugs

| ID | Issue Title | Evidence & Exact Locations | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BUG-CRIT-01** | **Governance Split-Brain (`localStorage` UI vs `portal_fee_settings` DB)** | `AppStore.saveFeeSettings()` (`src/services/store.ts:748-760`) writes ONLY to browser `localStorage` and dispatches a local DOM event. `RegistrationDonationControlPanel.tsx:101` and `AdminDashboard.tsx:952` never sync to PostgreSQL `public.system_settings.portal_fee_settings`. | **BUG-CRIT-02** | **Client vs Server Parent Fee Mismatch (`1500` vs `500` BDT)** | `src/utils/rules.ts:315` hardcodes `const parentFee = hasParent ? 1500 : 0`. `ManualRegistrationTab.tsx:1539, 1556` renders `+৳1,500`. ## 2. Critical Database & Security Vulnerabilities (Verified Live DB)

| ID | Vulnerability | Live Evidence & Migrations | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SEC-CRIT-01** | **Full Registration PII Exposed via `anon`-Executable RPCs** | `public.rpc_admin_get_registrations(integer)` (`audit_db_summary.txt:184`, `20270124` L135) and `public.get_paid_registrations()` (`20270106` L361) are `SECURITY DEFINER` with `anon`/default `PUBLIC` `EXECUTE` grants and zero role checks. | Any anonymous client can call PostgREST `/rest/v1/rpc/rpc_admin_get_registrations` to dump every row and PII column of `registrations_2027`, bypassing RLS! |
| **SEC-CRIT-02** | **`anon`-Executable Admin Deletion & Photo Mutation RPCs** | `public.rpc_delete_registration` (`audit_db_summary.txt:197`, `20270115` L8) and `public.rpc_update_student_photo` (`20270118` L77) remain `SECURITY DEFINER` callable by `anon` with no caller auth checks. | Anonymous callers can delete any registration or overwrite photo URLs. |
| **SEC-CRIT-03** | **All 6 Manual Registration RPCs Granted `TO anon` Without Caller Auth** | `rpc_create_manual_registration`, `rpc_staff_update_manual_registration`, both overloads of `rpc_approve_manual_registration`, `rpc_reject_manual_registration`, `rpc_approve_manual_financial`, `rpc_reject_manual_financial` (`audit_db_summary.txt:185-187, 195, 212-213, 221`, `20270309` L228–813). | Any anonymous caller can create manual registrations, forge `p_admin_name`, and self-approve rows straight into `registrations_2027`! |
| **SEC-CRIT-04** | **Open Audit Table Policies & Grants (`anon`)** | `admin_audit_history` has `GRANT ALL` and `FOR ALL TO anon USING (true)` (`audit_db_summary.txt:124, 238`, `20270131` L49–64); `admin_audit_logs` has `anon` `SELECT/INSERT` with `true` (`L127, 239-240`, `20270131` L67–85). | Anonymous users can inspect, forge, or delete governance/security audit logs directly via PostgREST. |
| **SEC-CRIT-05** | **Wide-Open RLS on `manual_registrations` & Legacy Tables** | `manual_registrations` has `qual=true / with_check=true` (`audit_db_summary.txt:305-306`); `manual_registration_audit_logs` retains `anon` grants (`L303-304`); `dress_distributions`, `food_distributions`, `gate_events`, `guest_*`, `financial_transactions`, `financial_audit_logs`, `registrations_2026` have permissive `qual=true` policies (`L258-316`). | Permissive `OR` policies bypass stricter staff/admin RLS rules for `authenticated` (and in some tables `anon`) roles. |
